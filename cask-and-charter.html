The Investment Case

An Asset That Improves With Every Year That Passes

Whisky cask investment gives you direct, legal ownership of a physical asset that naturally appreciates as it matures. Unlike stocks or property, there is no management hassle and no market correlation.

As part of a leading property consultancy group, Cask & Charter brings institutional rigour to a specialist asset class, giving private investors access to casks that never reach the open market.

Six Reasons to Invest

What Makes Whisky Cask Investment Different

1

Natural Appreciation

Every year your cask matures, the liquid becomes more complex and more sought after. Value builds with no action required from you.

2

Finite Supply

Scotch whisky cannot be rushed. A 15 year old malt cannot exist in fewer than 15 years. Supply is structurally limited while demand keeps growing.

3

Rising Global Demand

Asia, the Middle East, Latin America. New markets are driving unprecedented appetite for premium aged Scotch at a pace supply cannot match.

4

Tax Efficiency

Personal whisky casks may be classified as wasting assets under HMRC guidance, potentially outside Capital Gains Tax. Independent advice recommended.*

5

True Diversification

Whisky moves to its own rhythm. Uncorrelated to equities, bonds, or property. A genuine alternative within any sophisticated portfolio.

6

Proven Track Record

The Knight Frank Rare Whisky Icon 100 returned over 564% across the last decade, outperforming gold, art, wine, and major equity indices.

When You Are Ready to Sell

Your Exit Options

You are never locked into a single route. We guide you to whichever delivers the best return for your specific cask.

1

Whisky Auction

Scotland's premium auction houses attract global collectors and trade buyers. We manage the consignment, cataloguing, and settlement entirely on your behalf.

2

Direct Trade Sale

Our network of bottlers, blenders, and trade buyers creates a direct market for mature casks, often achieving returns above what public auction would deliver.

3

Private Bottling

Bottle your cask under your own label. A unique, collectible product with significant value that goes well beyond the liquid itself.

4

Discretionary Buy Back

In certain circumstances, Cask & Charter may offer a discretionary buy back on your cask. Speak to your account manager for full details.
